British History

Discover the most important events in the history of the United Kingdom

← Back to topic selection
1215
Magna Carta
King John sealed the Magna Carta at Runnymede, limiting royal power for the first time and laying the foundation for constitutional law.
1707
Act of Union
England and Scotland merged into a single Kingdom of Great Britain, joining their parliaments under one crown.
1837-1901
The Victorian Era
Under Queen Victoria, the Industrial Revolution transformed Britain into the world's leading economic power and largest empire.
1914-1918
World War I
Britain entered the Great War against Germany, suffering huge losses in the trenches of France and Belgium.
1939-1945
World War II
Britain stood alone against Nazi Germany during the Blitz, with Winston Churchill leading the country through its darkest hours to victory.
1948
Founding of the NHS
The National Health Service was founded, guaranteeing free healthcare for all UK residents regardless of income.
